TrendSpotter respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can contact us about your data.

We may collect your email address when you join early access, request a report, subscribe to Premium, or contact support. If you make a purchase, payment details are processed by Stripe. TrendSpotter does not store full card numbers.

How we use information

We use your information to provide TrendSpotter, send product and purchase emails, deliver reports, manage subscriptions, respond to support requests, and improve the service.

Service providers

We use trusted providers such as Stripe for payments, Render for hosting, Supabase for application data, and Resend for transactional email. These providers process data only as needed to support the service.

You can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information where applicable. Some billing records may need to be retained for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, or legal reasons.
