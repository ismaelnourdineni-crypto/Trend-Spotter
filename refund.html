Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

We want TrendSpotter purchases to be clear and fair. This policy explains how subscription cancellations and refund requests are handled.

Premium subscriptions

You can request cancellation of a Premium subscription by contacting support at hello@gettrendspotter.com. Once cancelled, access remains available until the end of the paid billing period unless otherwise stated.

Refund requests

Refund requests are reviewed case by case. If you were charged by mistake, cannot access the product, or believe there is a billing issue, contact us within 14 days of the charge.

Digital reports

Digital reports and downloadable research products may not be refundable once delivered, except where required by law or where there is a technical delivery issue that we cannot resolve.

How to contact us

Email hello@gettrendspotter.com with the email address used at checkout and any Stripe receipt or invoice reference.
